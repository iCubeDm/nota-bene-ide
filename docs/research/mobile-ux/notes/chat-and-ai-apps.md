# Chat-feed UX patterns in mobile messaging and AI-assistant apps (as of Nov 2026)

Method note: about 16 searches/fetches. Primary sources were reachable only for Telegram; the rest are search summaries of third-party articles, forum posts and teardowns (lower confidence). Failed fetches: NN/g article (404), Android Authority (403), TechRadar (truncated), claudeissues.com (unresolvable). Not covered, listed in Gaps rather than filled from memory: Signal, WhatsApp partial quote, unread markers, scroll-to-bottom, date separators, Le Chat, Copilot, streaming/stop button, tool-call display, evidence on chunked answers.

## Messaging apps: reply, long-press menus, reactions, partial quote, jump-to-message

**Takeaway.** Telegram (since Oct 2023) is the one messaging app with a confirmed, primary-sourced fragment reply: select text inside a message, choose "Quote", and tapping the quote later jumps to the exact spot. WhatsApp and Discord reply to a whole message by swipe or long press (Discord mobile has no swipe-to-reply). Slack added a long-press "Explain" action for dense messages.

**Findings**
- Telegram "Replies 2.0" (Oct 2023): tap the message (iOS: press and hold), press-hold and drag to select, choose "Quote". Several quotes per reply; quote formatting on any text. Tapping a quote navigates with animation to the exact place; a reply can go to another chat ("Reply in Another Chat"). The blog states no quote size limit (absence of information, not proof) ([Telegram](https://telegram.org/blog/reply-revolution)). Partial replies were requested for years; one request fixed in 10.1.0, secondary report Android 10.1.3+ ([tracker](https://bugs.telegram.org/c/1050/32), [SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/telegram-adds-quote-formatting-name-colors-revised-reply-more/)).
- WhatsApp: swipe right highlights and opens the reply field; long press also works and opens a reaction bar (six emoji plus "+"); double-tap to react added; group "Reply privately" under long press > More. All third-party ([Android Central](https://androidcentral.com/apps-software/whatsapp-is-making-it-a-snap-to-react-with-a-double-tap), [SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/862191/how-to-use-whatsapp-emoji-reactions/), [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/whatsapp-s-secret-swipe-feature-will-save-you-precious-time/story)). One unverified VoiceOver report: long-press menu closes before selection on iOS 26 ([AppleVis](https://www.applevis.com/comment/195702)).
- Discord mobile: long press, then Reply; reply ping on by default; users request swipe-to-reply citing WhatsApp/Telegram ([support](https://support.discord.com/hc/fr/articles/360057382374), [feedback](https://support.discord.com/hc/de/community/posts/4405717362967-Shortcut-to-Reply-to-Message-in-Mobile-Application)).
- Slack mobile: tap-and-hold shows desktop hover actions; Android adds Mark Unread and Star (2019, old) ([blog](https://slack.com/blog/productivity/get-more-from-slack-on-mobile)). Dec 2025 drop: refreshed action menus and a long-press "Explain" for jargon or dense messages ([blog](https://slack.com/blog/news/feature-drop-dec25)). Activity view (fall 2023, updated Jan 2026) collects DMs, mentions, threads, reactions with top tabs; swipe left clears an item ([Slack Design](https://slack.design/articles/no-small-task-evolving-the-way-millions-catch-up-in-slack/), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/slacks-latest-redesign-has-a-dedicated-dm-tab-and-a-discord-style-activity-view-130032154.html), [help](https://slack.com/intl/en-in/help/articles/46751260742035-Introducing-the-new-Activity-view-in-Slack)).
- Google Messages: long-press menu moved from a top toolbar to a floating list near the bubble ([Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/apps-software/google-messages-gets-a-major-gesture-and-menu-refresh)).

**Inferences**
- Telegram's flow (select, Quote, tap-to-jump) is the closest precedent for fragment comments; its entry is a text selection, not a bubble gesture.
- Whole-message swipe-to-reply is a fast path for short messages and does not scale to long multi-topic replies, matching the device-testing finding.
- Slack's "Explain" shows long press on a dense message can carry content-aware actions.

**Gaps.** No official source for WhatsApp, Signal, Slack, Discord on partial quote, bubble vs full-width layout, grouping, unread markers, scroll-to-bottom, date separators, sizes, gesture thresholds. Signal: nothing usable. Slack/Discord thread UI unverified.

## AI-assistant apps: rendering, long-answer structure, selection, quoting into the next prompt

**Takeaway.** No evidence that ChatGPT or Claude mobile support selecting a fragment and replying to it natively; it is repeatedly requested. Gemini has "Select text" and "Ask Gemini" in testing or rollout (unconfirmed). Perplexity is the strongest document-style answer, with inline citations and optional detail layers.

**Findings**
- OpenAI community: many requests for quote/reply to a passage; one says users "may need to copy and paste the text or explain which section they mean"; another asks for anchored annotations and contextual threads ([interactive selection](https://community.openai.com/t/interactive-text-selection/1413759), [anchored](https://community.openai.com/t/feature-request-inline-anchored-annotations-and-contextual-threads-for-chatgpt-responses/1390620), [quote-reply](https://community.openai.com/t/feature-suggestion-quote-reply-to-specific-text-segments/1378659)).
- A web "Ask ChatGPT" tooltip on highlight exists per posts; a bug report says the reference chip shows raw markdown. Not confirmed on mobile ([tooltips](https://community.openai.com/t/more-ask-chatgpt-tooltips-for-broswer-based-chatgpt-to-improve-ux/1380953), [bug](https://community.openai.com/t/unrendered-markdown-in-the-ask-chatgpt-reply-feature/1388930)). ChatGPT mobile selection complaints exist, title only seen ([forum](https://community.openai.com/t/iphone-air-users-is-chatgpt-text-selection-acting-weird-for-you-too/1378815)).
- ChatGPT branching: long press a message, "Branch in new chat" (labels vary); web first (Sept 2025), mobile around Jan 2026; secondary sources ([TechRadar](https://techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/chatgpt/the-chatgpt-app-just-got-a-big-upgrade-on-ios-and-android-to-stop-your-chats-spiraling-out-of-control), headline only; [post](https://groups.google.com/g/onecooltip/c/Vpt26NLriCI)).
- Claude: issue-tracker user reports say copy takes the whole message and users ask for selective copy; copy in Remote Control (Code tab) mobile sessions is reported broken (the same product class as this app); one iPad report: selecting in a long session auto-scrolls to top and deselects. User reports, not Anthropic docs ([75155](https://claudeissues.com/issue/75155-feature-request-selective-text-copy-within-messages-mobile-app-desktop-not-only), [71718](https://claudeissues.com/issue/71718-bug-cannot-copy-text-correctly-from-code-tab-remote-control-session-on-mobile-ap), [72190](https://claudeissues.com/issue/72190-ipados-app-selecting-text-in-a-long-remote-macos-session-auto-scrolls-to-top-and)).
- Gemini: Android Authority reported "Select text" to copy part of a response; an APK teardown reported "Ask Gemini" after selection; neither had a confirmed date. Web has "Modify selected text". Product Hunt (29 Jan 2026): no native quote-a-fragment, hence Chrome extensions ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/gemini-select-text-3436270), summary only; [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/quote-reply-for-gemini); [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/?p=608855)).
- Perplexity (teardowns): answer first, sources strip, related-question chips; inline numbered citations; collapsed "Reviewed sources"; expandable "Completed N steps"; action row share/copy/rewrite; highlighting reportedly offers "add to follow-up" and "check sources" (unverified). Criticisms: citation markers and source cards crowd small screens and push the answer down; all sources look equally authoritative; follow-up chips sometimes generic ([925 Studios](https://www.925studios.co/blog/perplexity-design-breakdown), [aiuxplayground](https://aiuxplayground.com/teardowns/perplexity/citations), [Crosley](https://blakecrosley.com/guides/design/perplexity)).

**Inferences.** The missing native fragment reply in ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini mobile is the pain device testing found, and request volume suggests demand; making fragment actions primary would differentiate. Perplexity's layers show sections of one answer can carry different affordances, a model for per-section actions.

**Gaps.** Per-answer actions (copy, regenerate, read aloud, share, edit) for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Le Chat; bubble vs full-width; streaming and stop behaviour; thinking/tool-call display; canvas/artifacts on mobile; Copilot and Le Chat entirely.

## Splitting one AI answer into several messages; evidence on chunked vs monolithic answers

**Takeaway.** No app found that splits one AI answer into separate messages, and no hard evidence comparing chunked with monolithic answers. Related: ChatGPT branching (conversation fork), Slack "Explain", Perplexity's section layers, requests for threaded replies.

**Findings**
- OpenAI community requests: threaded replies for long conversations; anchored annotations/contextual threads ([threaded](https://community.openai.com/t/threaded-replies-for-long-conversations/1385480), [anchored](https://community.openai.com/t/feature-request-inline-anchored-annotations-and-contextual-threads-for-chatgpt-responses/1390620)).
- Secondary summaries of NN/g chatbot research: the core problem is getting lost scrolling streams of replies, worse with lengthy outputs; the review of Google AI Mode lists long unformatted answers; advice is direct, scannable, easy-to-expand answers. Primary page not retrieved ([Experientia](https://blog.experientia.com/?p=20115), [Search Engine World](https://www.searchengineworld.com/influential-nielsen-norman-group-tees-off-on-google-ai-google-ai-mode), [NN/g author](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/author/georgia-kenderova/)).
- The study had 8 US participants (5 mobile, 3 desktop): small sample. A Jakob Nielsen summary of a ChatGPT study describes "apple-picking": scrolling back through long chats to find points ([substack](https://jakobnielsenphd.substack.com/p/ux-news-20230925)).

**Inferences.** Splitting is unproven but consistent with NN/g's scroll-and-lose-place findings and the anchored/threaded requests; it needs its own testing. Chunking at generation time vs render time (by heading/paragraph/tool call) is a design choice the research does not settle.

## Known complaints and praised patterns

- Complaints: verbosity and scrolling (Reddit Mar 2026: walls of text for simple questions; "answers too long, verbose" 115 points, 52 comments; workaround is prompt length limits; thin sourcing; OpenAI reportedly made answers more direct) ([Reddit](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1rxxltp/snapshots/2026-03-20T14%3A50%3A06.156883Z), [ai-productivity](https://ai-productivity.pages.dev/news/chatgpt-verbosity-complaints-growing-2026/)); selection/copy friction in Claude mobile sessions (75155 above); citation density on mobile (Perplexity).
- Praised: Telegram quote with tap-to-jump ([AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2023/10/telegram-rolls-out-major-update-with-improved-replies-and-enhanced-stories)); Perplexity's answer-first layering.
- Inference: selection is the weakest part of mobile AI chat; a design that does not rely on native OS text selection (explicit per-paragraph targets) avoids a known pain point.
- Gap: no systematic app-store review analysis of message-action UX.
