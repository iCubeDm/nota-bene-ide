Nota Bene for Android: every flow as implemented

A review board of the phone app, in the spirit of a Figma page: each screen is drawn from the code at main 5a27066 (after #176 and #178), Darcula theme, English interface, 360 dp wide. Captions are in English. Arrows name the gesture that leads to the next screen. Tags under a screen point to the gap analysis in report.md (numbers in brackets are the weak points of notes/our-app-inventory.md). Dashed red frames mark the spot a tag is about. Drawings approximate spacing; texts, order of controls and states are exact.

14. Proposed: header presence and context ring review-1 items 4, 7, 8, 9

The messenger principle: the second line of the header pill says what the other side is doing, as “typing…” and “last seen” do. The status bar under the tabs goes away in every state. The free space right of the pill holds a context ring; a tap on it opens the mode menu (a menu, not a cycle, so a stray tap cannot switch to Auto or full access).

agent starts
tap the ring
window restarts

15. Proposed: comment by paragraph, Pin, labels review-1 items 5, 6 and the decisions

A tap on a paragraph of a final reply makes it the target. Above it: one bubble of quick labels, as Telegram's reactions; below it: an action bar. Selecting part of the text is a step inside the paragraph, not the way in. Links, code blocks and diagrams keep their own taps; a long-press keeps the whole-message menu (7.1) for Copy and Reply.
